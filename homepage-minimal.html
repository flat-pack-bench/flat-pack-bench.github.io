Flat-Pack Bench

Evaluating Spatio-Temporal Understanding in Large Vision-Language Models through Furniture Assembly

Aditya Chetan, Eric Cai, Peeyush Kushwaha, Bharath Raj Nagoor Kani, Utkarsh Mall, Qianqian Wang, Noah Snavely, Bharath Hariharan

CVPR 2026

Abstract

Flat-Pack Bench is a multiple-choice benchmark for fine-grained spatio-temporal video understanding. Each question asks a model to reason about parts, contact events, assembly order, final connectivity, or object identity across time in real furniture assembly videos.

PDF Data Viewer Results Citation

Benchmark

The benchmark contains 602 questions across 50 real assembly videos and four question families: temporal localization, temporal ordering, mating, and tracking.

Temporal localization Find first, last, next, or most recent contact events.
Temporal ordering Recover the order of part and part-pair attachments.
Mating Determine whether two labeled parts connect in the final assembly.
Tracking Match labeled parts across states despite motion and occlusion.
