Flat-Pack Bench asks models to understand when parts move, touch, connect, and stay identical across real assembly videos. The first thing visitors see here is the benchmark setup, not a decorative hero.

PDF Data Viewer Results Citation

What the benchmark tests

Assembly videos expose temporal and object-centric reasoning failures that are easy to miss in static image benchmarks. Flat-Pack Bench focuses on contact events, part identity, assembly order, and final connectivity.
